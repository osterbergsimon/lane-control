## Away-improve: what is expected of you
The owner is away and has switched on improve mode so that this project keeps getting better on its own. You are
expected to improve the product, not only fix bugs. New features, better UX, performance, research and bold ideas
are welcome. Bigger changes are allowed when they are clearly better and explain themselves: say what is better for
the user and why, in the PR body (its log entry). Check what exists first and build on it: read the code, the open and
recently closed issues, the open and recently merged PRs, the docs and the merged away items before you design anything.

Three things count as failure even when the work runs:
- a whole area rewritten from scratch (the frontend, say); "it is cleaner" is not a reason, a measured or visible
  gain for the product is;
- an owner who cannot understand what happened: the log entry must be plain, short and enough to follow without you;
- work that duplicates something that already exists because nobody looked.
